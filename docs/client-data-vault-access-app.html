The method

Three movements, repeated for every source.

New systems are added through a standard onboarding pattern — not a custom build each time. The same model carries every domain.

01 · INGEST

We ingest.

Any operational system with an API or data feed — transactions, movements, people, customers, assets, events. Onboarding new sources is a configuration job, not a code project.

02 · CONFORM

We conform.

Every source feeds the same standardised entity model. A product, location or transaction means the same thing regardless of where it came from — and the model absorbs new business domains as your operation evolves.

03 · QUERY

You query.

Connect Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Metabase, Python or dbt — anything that speaks SQL — directly to your dedicated database. No middleware, no exports, no waiting for IT.

The model

One entity model, six business domains — and room for the next one.

Hubs are the things your business runs on (Product, Location, Customer). Events are what happens, with a timestamp. Domains plug in without rebuilding the model.

Hub — a thing your business runs on Event — something that happened, with a timestamp Domain — a business area; new ones plug in
Connect

Bring your own tool.

Every organisation lives in its own SQL Server database. You get a scoped, read-only login against the datavault and presentation schemas. The pipeline keeps writing on its schedule; nothing your queries do can affect it.

Data lands on a recurring cadence. Every record carries a load timestamp, so freshness is always visible in your queries — and the Data Vault never forgets, with effective-from / effective-to dates on every change.
