    RECONCILED 2026-07-31 by executing the build read-only against this org:
      31 May  F_COGS_PERIOD CLOSING_VALUE = 10,151.06 vs F_INV_COUNTS_DAY
              10,175.54. The GBP 24.48 gap is NOT an error in the new fact -
              F_INV_COUNTS_DAY holds 158 rows for 157 distinct items on that
              date, double-counting one item worth GBP 24.48 twice.
              10,175.54 - 24.48 = 10,151.06 exactly. F_COGS_PERIOD's grain is
              unique (732 rows / 732 distinct keys) so it counts it once.
              The duplicate in the LIVE F_INV_COUNTS_DAY is a separate
              pre-existing defect worth raising on its own.
      30 Jun  F_COGS_PERIOD 8,496.68 vs F_INV_COUNTS_DAY 8,127.01. Expected
              and correct: 220 items were not counted at the 30 Jun
              stocktake, so this fact carries their May counts forward by
              design, while F_INV_COUNTS_DAY only values the 150 items
              actually counted that day.
  - KNOWN SOURCE-DATA CAVEAT ON THIS ORG, read before showing anyone the
    Variance KPI: Growyze sends only COUNT and ORDER events here - there are
    NO WASTE, SALE, TRANSFER or PRODUCTION events at all. So WASTE_VALUE is
    0.00 on every row and VARIANCE_VALUE comes out as exactly -COG_SOLD
    (June: -3,387.77). That is arithmetically correct given the inputs, but
    it means the "unexplained shrink" KPI is reporting the entire
    consumption as unexplained, because nothing in the feed explains any of
    it. This is a source-data condition, not a build defect - but do not
    present that KPI as a shrink figure on this org.
  - Do NOT use Padel Social or Dirty Sixth for value reconciliation. Both have
    more count dates but implausible stock values (roughly GBP 587k-1.2M) from
    a separate, unresolved cause (see PREFLIGHT.md background / open item O5).
    Reconciling against either would validate the new dashboard against
    numbers that are already known to be wrong.
